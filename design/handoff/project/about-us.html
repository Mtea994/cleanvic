How We Started

From One Van to
Victoria's #1 Cleaning Brand

KleanVictoria was founded by Michael Tran, a former hotel housekeeping manager who was frustrated by how poorly most cleaning companies treated both their customers and their staff. He saw an industry full of shortcuts, hidden fees, and unreliable contractors — and decided to build something different.

Starting with a single van and a small crew of trusted friends, Michael built KleanVictoria on three principles: show up on time, do the job properly, and be honest about pricing. Word spread fast. Within two years, the business had expanded to 12 team members and 400+ regular clients across Melbourne.

Today, KleanVictoria operates across all of Victoria with 32 full-time cleaners, a dedicated operations team, and a 4.9-star rating across 620+ reviews. But the founding principles haven't changed at all.

"I started this business because I wanted to be the cleaner I would actually hire myself. Someone who turns up, does a brilliant job, and charges a fair price. That's still what we do every day."

— Michael Tran, Founder & Director
What We Stand For

Our Values, In Practice

Not just words on a wall — principles that guide every single job we do.

Uncompromising Quality

Every job is treated as if we're cleaning for our own family. We don't sign off unless the result is something we're proud of — and neither should you.

Radical Transparency

No hidden charges. No surprise fees. You get a clear, itemised quote before we start — and that's the price you pay. Full stop.

People First

Our cleaners are paid well above industry rates, trained properly, and treated with respect. Happy team members do better work — it's that simple.

Eco-Responsibility

We use hospital-grade, biodegradable products. Safe for children, pets, and waterways. We actively reduce single-use plastic in all our operations.

Trust & Safety

Every team member is police-checked before they enter your home. We carry $10M public liability insurance. Your safety is never negotiable.

Reliability Always

We show up when we say we will. If we're ever delayed, you'll know before we're due to arrive — not when you're already waiting at home.
