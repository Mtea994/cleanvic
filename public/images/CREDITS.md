## Optimisation rules

Every photo committed here should be:
- Long edge ≤ 2000px (heroes 1920px, before/after 1200px, region cards 1200px)
- JPEG quality ~80%; prefer WebP at q80 for ~30% smaller files at equal quality
- `og-default.png` is the only fixed-size asset: **1200×630 exact** (OG spec)
- `melbourne-coverage-map.png` should be PNG or WebP with transparency so the navy page background shows through
- Sub-folder organised by purpose if the count grows beyond 30 files
- Accompanied by alt text drafted in the page consuming the image

## Curation guidance

- Avoid the most-overused "smiling cleaner with spray bottle" stock shots.
- Favour honest, action-oriented imagery — a vacuum mid-stroke, a window
  with a squeegee streak, an actual Melbourne terrace exterior.
- Avoid heavily-staged corporate stock — reads as fake to AU residential
  consumers.
- Consider swapping for owner-supplied job photos as soon as they are
  available; stock is a launch fallback, not a permanent solution.
